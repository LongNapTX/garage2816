Our Story

Garage 2816 was created from a lifelong passion for motorcycles and an appreciation for the engineering, history, and individuality that make each motorcycle special.

Located in Rosenberg, Texas, Garage 2816 focuses on finding rare, unique, and hard-to-find used motorcycles. We believe that motorcycles are more than transportation. They represent craftsmanship, performance, and personal history.

Our goal is to offer carefully selected motorcycles while preserving their original appearance and factory specifications whenever possible. We want every customer to feel confident and excited about their purchase.

What Makes Us Different

  • Passion for motorcycles and their history
  • Specialization in unique and rare models
  • Attention to originality and authenticity
  • Personalized customer service
  • Convenient motorcycle delivery and inspection

Our Passion for Motorcycles

From vintage classics to modern performance motorcycles, we appreciate the unique characteristics of every machine.
