About Us

Garage 2816 specializes in rare, unique, and hard-to-find used motorcycles. Each motorcycle is carefully selected, thoroughly cleaned, and reconditioned, emphasizing original factory specifications and preserving its authenticity.

The Buying Experience

Our mission is to provide an exceptional, white-glove motorcycle buying experience through a virtual showroom featuring high-resolution photography and video.

Customers can select one or more motorcycles and place a nonrefundable deposit for delivery and inspection at their location. If purchased, the deposit applies toward the purchase price, with financing and paperwork completed on-site. Otherwise, the deposit covers round-trip transportation costs.

Why Choose Garage 2816?

Our goal is to make buying a unique motorcycle convenient and enjoyable.

  • Rare and hard-to-find motorcycles
  • Carefully selected inventory
  • Factory-original appearance and authenticity
  • High-resolution photography and video
  • Personalized delivery and inspection
